Colony Builders scores hauling to colonisation projects: a point per tonne delivered, doubled for squadron priority builds, with a bonus for the delivery that finishes a commodity.

The exact arithmetic:

- 1 point per tonne delivered to a tracked project.
- ×2 when the project is the squadron's current effort (the priority flag) at scoring time.
- +500 for closing a commodity line — being the latest delivery on a commodity whose remaining need now reads zero. If a line reopens (the site asks for more) and somebody finishes it again, the bonus pays again: somebody closed it again.

Deliveries are recorded from the game's own journal via the companion app, so only attributed deliveries score — hauling without the app paired earns nothing on the board. Deliveries to projects that have since completed still count.

The page shows "Season {month}" standings, the "All-time" roll and "The tier ladder", with columns "#", "Runner", "Points" and "Deliveries", plus your own "Season rank" / "This season" / "Lifetime" strip.

The Colony Builders ranks, by lifetime points on this board:

- "Bricklayer" at 1,000
- "Foreman" at 10,000
- "Architect" at 50,000
- "Worldshaper" at 200,000

The board's achievement badges: "First Brick" (a first delivery to a colonisation project), "Priority Hauler" (10,000 tonnes delivered to squadron priority builds), "Line Closer" (made the delivery that finished a commodity — the last tonne of something), and "Colony Builder Champion" (topped the board for a whole season).

Scoring runs every few minutes, so a delivery shows on the board shortly after it lands on the project page. Your visibility on the board is the "Colony Builders" switch in Commander Mgmt; points accrue regardless.
