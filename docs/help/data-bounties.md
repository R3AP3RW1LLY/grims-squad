Data Bounties pays you for lighting up dark stations. Every station on the board has market data past the ninety-day believability band — or none at all. Dock at one with the companion app paired and open the commodities screen: the upload refreshes the squadron's data and banks the bounty automatically. Staler pays more, jackpots pay double, and each bounty pays exactly once.

There is no claim button, on the board or anywhere else:

1. Pick a station from the board under "Answer the Call" → "Data Bounties".
2. Fly there, dock, and open the commodity market with the app running.
3. Credit lands the moment the market upload arrives. You are notified: "Data bounty banked: {station}" with the points — "a jackpot claim" appended when it was one. Jackpots are announced to the whole squadron.

First come, first paid: whoever's market snapshot lands first takes the bounty, and a second member docking five minutes later finds it gone. A claimed station simply disappears from the board.

The board has two sections. "Squadron space — within 200 ly of our active projects" is the space the squadron actually flies, anchored on live colonisation projects; only here do jackpots exist. "The galaxy tail — the stalest data we hold anywhere" lists the darkest data in the galaxy, no jackpots. Columns cover the station, system, type, pads, distance from ops, "Data age" ("never seen", "120d dark", "2.1y dark") and points, with a "Jackpot ×2" chip where it applies. Fleet carriers are excluded — a carrier moves, so its data going dark means nothing.

The board rebuilds every half hour ("Board refreshed {n} min ago" at the top), holding up to 300 squadron-space and 200 galaxy-tail rows. A new bounty can appear up to thirty minutes late; payment is never late, because a claim consumes its row instantly.

One honest edge: claims ride the "Market screens you open" telemetry event. Switching off the "Trade and mining" category stops your uploads carrying it, so no bounty can bank.
