title: Marking your current build
surface: both
route: /colonisation/squadron

Your current build is the one colonisation project you are actively hauling to right now. Marking it is what makes the companion app's build tracker overlay follow that project — and what routes "line closed" notices to you.

1. Open the project and join it from the "Crew" tab ("JOIN THIS BUILD").
2. Tick the checkbox "This is my current build". It sits with the leave button, and appears only once you are on the roster.

You have exactly one current build: ticking the box on a different project moves the mark rather than adding a second. Unticking it clears it. A finished build refuses the mark with "That build is already finished."

What it drives:

- The "Build tracker" overlay shows this project's needs and progress wherever you fly — refreshed from the hub every minute, or straight from the game's depot reading while you are docked at the site itself.
- The "Cargo hold" overlay highlights commodities this build still needs, so a hold being loaded three systems away is checked against the right list.
- When somebody delivers the last tonnes of a commodity on this project, members with it marked as their current build are notified: "{commodity} is covered on {title}" — "The last tonnes of it have been delivered. Check the needs list before your next load." The message goes to current-build holders, not the whole crew, and never to the person who closed the line.

On the roster, crewmates with the project marked show an "on this build" tag against their name. If the marked project is later closed, deleted or made invisible to you, the overlay simply returns to its empty prompt — you are never trapped on a build you cannot see.
