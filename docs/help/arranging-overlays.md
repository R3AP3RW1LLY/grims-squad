---
title: Switching on and arranging the in-game overlays
surface: companion
route: Settings
---
The "Overlays" tab in the app's Settings controls four in-game panels: "Build tracker", "Trade run", "Cargo hold" and "Upload status". Each is its own small always-on-top window.

To set them up:

1. In "Panels", tick the checkbox on each overlay you want. The "Style" button on a card opens its controls: "Opacity" (20–100%), "Size" (0.70–2.00×), "Accent" colour, "Where it goes" ("Follow the game", "Over the game", or "Its own window (second screen)"), and "What it shows" — per-field checkboxes.
2. In "Arrange", press "Arrange on screen". Every overlay now takes the mouse — drag them by the header (it reads "drag · resize edges") and resize by the edges.
3. Press "Done arranging". They lock again automatically.

Locked overlays pass your clicks straight through to the game — that is why there is no per-panel unlock; arranging is the one moment they take the mouse. Positions save when you let go, and a panel that ends up off-screen after a monitor change is pulled back onto a real display on its own.

Where they can draw is decided by Elite's display mode. In Borderless or Windowed, panels sit over the game; over-game panels hide whenever Elite is not the foreground window, and everything hides when Elite is not running. In true Fullscreen, Windows will not draw anything over the game, so panels open in their own windows instead — even if you picked "Over the game" — and a note at the top of the tab explains it. "Its own window (second screen)" keeps a panel in a normal window that stays up while Elite runs, which suits a second monitor.

"Upload status" (on screen: "UPLINK") shows a "Sending" or "Idle" dot, "Last upload", and whether Elite is running. "Trade run" shows "Pick a run in the Freight Office." until a run is picked.
