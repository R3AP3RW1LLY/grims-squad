"Build types" is the reference for what every kind of construction site asks for — the thing to read before committing to post one. Pick a row to see its full bill of materials and what it would cost to buy near you.

The table's columns are "Build", "Tier", "Where", "Pad", "To haul", "Commodities" and "Figures". Filter with "Find" (matching name and category — "coriolis, settlement…"), and the "Tier", "Where" and "Pad" selects; the footer counts what matches.

The "Figures" column is the honesty column. Frontier publishes none of these numbers — they were gathered by players and disagree at the edges — so every row says where its figures came from:

- "measured" (with "×{n}" when several sites confirm it): "Matched exactly by {n} of our own construction sites". A row earns this automatically when a squadron construction site's bill of materials matches it commodity for commodity, tonnage for tonnage — which happens the first time somebody with the companion app docks at a site of that type.
- "unchecked": "A community figure. Nobody has checked it against one of our own builds yet."

The "How much of this is measured" panel keeps the running score, and it goes up every time somebody docks at a site the squadron has not seen before.

A build's detail page shows four tiles ("To haul", "Commodities", "Cost near you", "Figures") and:

1. "What it does to a system" — the seven system-effect figures and what economy it feeds.
2. "Price it from" — type a system into "Buying from" and press "Price it". Every commodity is priced against the nearest seller within 100 ly, fleet carriers excluded. Without a system there is nothing honest to quote. In the companion app this field starts as the system you are docked at.
3. "What it asks for" — the commodity table with tonnes, nearest source, price and cost, and a total: "About {n} cr in cargo, bought near {system}." Lines nobody in range sells are counted separately so the total never pretends to be complete.
4. "Also known as" — the other names the game and community lists use.
