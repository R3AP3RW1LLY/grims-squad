The forum is where the squadron talks when it is not in Discord — the things worth keeping, and finding again six months later. Boards are permission-gated: the index shows what you can see, the rail counts what you can post in, and a board you cannot post in says so — "You can read this board but not post in it."

To start a thread:

1. Open a board and press "New thread".
2. Give it a "Title" (3–200 characters) and write the "Opening post". A live "PREVIEW" beside the composer shows exactly how it will look once posted.
3. Press "Start the thread".

Replies work the same way under a thread ("REPLY", then "Post reply"). Pressing "Quote" or "Reply" on a post targets it — a "Replying to {name}" chip appears, with "CLEAR" to untarget — and the person you replied to is notified.

The editor is rich text: bold, italics, headings, alignment, lists, quotes, code, dividers, links, a font picker, images (uploaded straight into the post, with alt text, captions and width control) and YouTube videos — pasting a YouTube link embeds it. In replies, typing "@" mentions somebody, and they are notified.

You can edit your own posts at any time — there is no time limit — and edits made after the first few minutes carry an "· edited" marker. Every edit passes through the same screening as a new post. On boards that hold the squadron's documentation, maintainers can also edit guides they did not write.

Locked threads are read-only and say so: "This thread is read-only. If something here is wrong or you are stuck on a step, post in the help board — somebody will see it there." Pinned threads sit in their own "PINNED" group at the top of a board. Boards you have not looked at since somebody posted carry an unread pill; opening the board clears it.
