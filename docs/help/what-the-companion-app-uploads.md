title: What the companion app uploads, and switching parts off

The companion app reads your Elite Dangerous journals and uploads a defined list of events — nothing outside it. The "What is collected" section on the website's "Companion app" page shows the whole list, grouped, with a plain sentence per event saying what it actually reveals. Everything is kept unless you switch it off, and switching something off also deletes what has already been stored under it.

The groups: "Playing", "Ranks and standing", "Ships", "Where you are", "Combat", "Trade and mining", "Exploration", "Background simulation", "Fleet carrier", "On foot", and "Colonisation". Each group toggles as a whole, shows "On" or "Off", and its individual events can be switched off one by one while the group is on. Turning a group back on clears its per-event switch-offs, so a forgotten row cannot silently override your choice.

One group cannot be switched off: "Playing", marked "Always on", whose rows read "Required". It records that you played at all, and when — the single input the monthly promotion check reads. Switching it off would silently stop you qualifying for promotions, so the server refuses it rather than allowing it. Your credit balance rides with its "Game start" event, but is shown to others only if "Show my credit balance" is on.

Notable rows: "Market screens you open" shares the prices you saw with the squadron so route-finding works from real sightings — your name is not attached to them. The "Colonisation" group is what keeps a project you post up to date.

Two honest edges. Settings are applied on the server, so something switched off does travel from your machine before it is discarded and deleted. And private chat, your friends list and message contents are never transmitted at all — they contain other people's words, and nobody can consent to that on their behalf.

Saving reports what happened: "Saved.", or "Saved. {n} stored events were deleted."
