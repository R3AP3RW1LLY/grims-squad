Commander Mgmt, under the personal section of the sidebar, is where your account lives. The page is one place with five tabs, so answering "how is my account set up" does not take five page loads.

The tabs, in order:

1. "Commander settings" — how the hub shows things to you. "Times and dates" holds the "Your timezone" picker, and "What others can see" holds the privacy switches and the "Leaderboards" participation switches. The rail shows your commander name, timezone, "Fields shared" count, and the state of your companion app (including "v{yours} — v{latest} available" when an update is out).
2. "Name & verification" — Inara verification: the "Inara API key" field, "Verify my commander", your verification state, and (once you have a Discord name on record) the "Your name in Discord" section.
3. "Security" — the authenticator. Signing in takes one step, your Discord account; a second factor is required only to open the admin console. Here you can enrol ("Set up two-factor"), move to a new authenticator, or remove it.
4. "Forum signature" — how you appear on the forums, with two ways in: "GENERATE WITH GMSD AI" ("Design mine") or "BUILD IT MYSELF" ("Start building").
5. "Account" — "Signed-in devices" with an "End session" button per session, and "Your data" with "Download my data", which exports everything the hub holds about you as a JSON file: profile, privacy settings, Discord link, roles, verified commander names, activity totals and sessions.

The companion app has its own page ("Companion app" in the sidebar) rather than a tab here — it is software you download, not a setting.
