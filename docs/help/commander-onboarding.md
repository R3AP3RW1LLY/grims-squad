title: What happens after your first sign-in
surface: website
route: /onboarding/commander
---
Your first sign-in walks you through a short setup, one page at a time. Until it is done, hub pages redirect you to your next step, so you cannot lose your place.

The steps run in this order:

1. Authenticator (accounts with elevated permissions only): officers are taken to a security page first to enrol an authenticator app before anything else.
2. "SET UP YOUR COMMANDER": one question — "Your timezone", with a live clock preview so you can confirm the pick. Press "Save and continue". Every time on the site renders in this zone.
3. "THE COMPANION APP": explains what the app sends and how to connect it. "Get the app" opens the download page in a new tab; "Continue to the site" takes you onward. Seeing this page completes the step.
4. Verification: the "WAITING ON VERIFICATION" page, with "TWO WAYS THROUGH" — ask an officer in Discord to confirm your commander name (takes them about ten seconds), or link an Inara API key, which is automatic and immediate. Officers themselves are not held at this step.
5. "YOUR NAME IN DISCORD" (shown to members permitted to choose their own nickname): explains the squadron's naming style and lets you set a different Discord name. Everyone else wears their verified commander name automatically.

After the walk-through you land on your dashboard. Everything set here can be changed later: timezone and verification live in Commander Mgmt, and the companion app has its own page under your personal section of the sidebar.
