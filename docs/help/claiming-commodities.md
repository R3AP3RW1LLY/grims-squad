title: Claiming commodities on a build

Claims are how a crew divides the hauling without two people fetching the same pile. They live on a project's "Crew" tab.

Under the roster sits the uncovered list: "Nobody is covering these yet — take a share and it shows against your name." It lists every commodity still needed that no crew member has claimed, with the tonnes left. Each line has three buttons:

1. "¼" — claim a quarter of what remains.
2. "½" — claim half.
3. "All" — claim the lot.

Each button shows the worked-out tonnage beside it, rounded up, so three quarter-shares genuinely cover a pile. Claiming again on the same commodity corrects your figure rather than stacking a second claim.

A claim is visible intent, not a lock. It appears under your name on the roster and takes the commodity off the uncovered list for everyone — but it reserves nothing: the site's needs keep updating from the depot as cargo lands, whoever delivers it. Claims have no expiry; they stand until you press "Drop" on the roster, claim over them, or leave the build (leaving drops all your claims — your deliveries stay).

Work can also be assigned. On a squadron project, officers can put a commodity against a member's name; on a personal project, only the poster can — rank does not override a personal build. Assigned lines carry an "(assigned)" suffix on the roster, and being assigned puts you on the crew if you were not already. You can always drop your own line, whoever assigned it.

The claim tonnage is guidance for the crew; the "Where to buy" tab's numbers subtract attached carriers' holds, never claims.
