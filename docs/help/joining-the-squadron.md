The "JOIN GRIM'S SQUAD NOW" button on the public site puts you into the squadron's Discord server. It is a different door from "SQUADRON PORTAL SIGN IN": sign-in is for people already in the server, and this flow exists to put newcomers in.

1. Press "JOIN GRIM'S SQUAD NOW" in the top bar, or "JOIN GRIM'S SQUAD" on the home page.
2. On the "JOIN THE SERVER" page, pick one of the two cards: "JOIN THE SQUADRON" ("I want to fly with Grim's Squad.") or "ALLY OR OBSERVER" ("I am from an allied squadron, or I just want to watch.").
3. Press "CONTINUE WITH DISCORD" on your card.
4. Discord asks you to approve two things: your basic profile, and permission to add you to this one server. The site does not request your email address, cannot read your messages, and cannot see what other servers you are in.
5. You are added to the Discord server with the matching role — squadron joiners get the members role, allies get the Allies role — and land on a welcome page ("WELCOME, CMDR" for squadron joiners, "FLY SAFE, ALLY" for allies) with two buttons: "OPEN DISCORD" and "BACK TO THE HUB".

If you were already in the server, the flow recognises that and the welcome page greets you as already aboard rather than treating you as new.

Once you are in the Discord server, "SQUADRON PORTAL SIGN IN" works, and your first sign-in walks you through commander setup.
