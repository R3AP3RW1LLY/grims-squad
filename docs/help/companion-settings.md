"Settings", at the bottom of the app's sidebar, has two tabs: "This device" and "Overlays".

On "This device":

- The "This device" card shows "Paired as {token}" with a redacted token, and three buttons: "Open the hub" (opens the website in your browser), "Find my journals again" (re-runs the journal search — use it if your Saved Games folder moved), and "Unpair this device".
- "Starting up" has one checkbox: "Start with Windows, minimised to the tray".
- "What the squadron keeps" points you at the website: every category the app collects — including colonisation — can be switched off individually on the hub, and it says exactly what each one reveals. "Manage on the hub" takes you there.

The app finds journals on its own: it looks in the standard Saved Games\Frontier Developments\Elite Dangerous folder, including OneDrive-relocated copies on Windows and the Proton, CrossOver and Whisky locations on Linux and macOS. If nothing is found, it tells you what to try — usually "Start Elite Dangerous once and quit, then try again."

Closing the window does not quit the app: it lives in your system tray ("It lives in your system tray. Right-click the icon there to pause it or quit properly."), where the menu offers "Open", "Pause sending" / "Resume sending", and "Quit". With autostart on, it launches hidden at sign-in and starts watching straight away.

The app keeps itself up to date: it checks for a new release hourly, downloads it silently, and installs it while Elite is not running — retrying each minute until the game closes. Your pairing and settings are kept.

The "Overlays" tab is where the in-game overlays are switched on, styled and arranged; it has its own article.
