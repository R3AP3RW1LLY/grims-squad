---
title: Verifying your commander with Inara
surface: website
route: /settings/commander
---
Verification answers two questions, both from Inara: which commander is yours, and whether you fly with Grim's Squad. You never type either — the site reads them from your Inara account, and your Discord nickname is kept matching.

To verify with an Inara key:

1. Open Commander Mgmt, tab "Name & verification".
2. On Inara, open your profile menu, then "API keys", and generate one.
3. Paste it into the "Inara API key" field and press "Verify my commander".
4. On success you see "Verified as CMDR {name}. Your Discord nickname now matches."

The key is encrypted before it is stored and never shown again — not to you, not to an officer. With a key on file the panel shows an "On file" chip, "Last checked" date, and two buttons: "Re-check now" and "Remove key". Removing the key keeps your verification: "Key removed. Your commander name stays verified."

The other route: say hello in Discord and tell an officer your commander name. Any officer can confirm it.

The three states you can be in:

- "Not verified" — nothing confirmed yet.
- "Partially verified" — your name is proven, but Inara does not yet show you in the squadron. The panel walks you through it: apply to the squadron on Inara, wait for an officer to accept the application there, then tick "I have applied to join {squadron} on Inara." The tick verifies nothing by itself — it tells the site to start checking Inara, and to keep checking automatically until Inara shows you in. You do not need to stay on the page.
- "Verified commander" — both halves confirmed. The roster badge "Inara verified" needs this full state.

If Inara does not recognise the key, the message says to copy it again from your Inara profile settings. If another member already verified that commander name, speak to an officer. A failed re-check never removes an existing verification.
