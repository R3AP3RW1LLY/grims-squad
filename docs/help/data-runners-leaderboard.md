Data Runners ranks members by Data Bounty points: docking at stations whose market data has gone dark, staler pays more, jackpots pay double.

The page has three parts. "Season {month}" is this month's standings; "All-time" is the lifetime roll; "The tier ladder" shows the ranks and where you stand on them. Tables list "#", "Runner", "Points" and "Claims", top fifty. Your own strip above the tables shows "Season rank", "This season" and "Lifetime" — it appears with your first points.

Move between seasons with the arrow links beside the month; each season is its own URL, so a month's standings can be pasted into Discord and everybody sees the same table.

The Data Runners ranks, by lifetime points on this board:

- "Signal Scout" at 500
- "Wayfinder" at 5,000
- "Beacon Keeper" at 25,000
- "Void Cartographer" at 100,000

Each rank pays out as a badge, alongside the board's achievements: "First Light" (banked a first data bounty), "Jackpot Hunter" (ten jackpot claims), "Cartographer" (claimed a never-seen station — the squadron held nothing at all until you docked), and "Data Runner Champion" (topped the board for a whole season).

The "Data Bounties" page also carries its own "Data Runner leaderboard" section with season and all-time tabs and a jackpots column, right next to the bounty board that feeds it.

Points come only from bounty claims, which require the companion app paired — the board can hold no anonymous scores. Whether your name appears at all is your choice in Commander Mgmt's "Leaderboards" switches; hidden members keep earning points and still see their own strip.
