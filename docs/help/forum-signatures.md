Your signature appears under everything you post on the forum. The "Forum signature" tab in Commander Mgmt offers two ways to make one:

- "GENERATE WITH GMSD AI" ("Design mine"): answer four questions — your activity, your ship, the vibe, and anything in your own words — and it designs five signatures from your answers, drawing artwork for the backplates. Use one as it is, or open it in the builder.
- "BUILD IT MYSELF" ("Start building"): every colour, layer, font and image, in four steps — "Avatar", "Words", "Banner", "Finish" — with a live preview beside you the whole way.

The pieces: an avatar (upload one, or "USE MY DISCORD PHOTO" — the signature avatar shows on the forum only), a "TAGLINE" of up to 120 characters, a banner link to an allowed site (Inara, EDSM, Twitch, YouTube, Kick, the game's own site) with a 60-character label, and the banner itself — 600×160, three rows, up to 18 layers of text and badges over a solid, gradient, starfield or image background, any hex colour with the squadron palette offered as presets.

Banner text layers can draw from live sources — your commander name, squadron rank, pilot ranks, current ship, member-since date — resolved when the banner is drawn, so a promotion updates your signature by itself. Toggles control the rest: "Show my signature under my posts", "Show my squadron rank", "Show my commander name". "Save signature" saves it all.

To use the banner on other forums, press "Publish for other forums". Your banner here stays live and updates itself; a published copy is a snapshot, so re-publish after a promotion or a redesign. "SHARE IT ELSEWHERE" then offers four formats: "BBCode" (most forums), "Markdown" (Discord, GitHub), "HTML", and a plain "Image URL".

Readers who prefer plain text can ignore everybody's fonts with "Show all posts in the site font" in their own settings — that changes nothing about how your posts look to anyone else.
