---
title: The notification bell, and what rings it
surface: website
route: /dashboard
---
The bell sits top-right, beside your account menu, wearing the count of everything unread (capped at "99+"). It opens a panel with two tabs, "Personal" and "Squadron", each with its own unread pill.

"Personal" is about you, one row per event, each individually read or unread. What lands there: replies to your posts, "@" mentions, new replies in threads you follow, "Your answer was accepted", moderation outcomes on your held posts, "Badge earned: {name}", "Data bounty banked: {station}", commodities covered on your current build, joins and leaves on projects you posted, "Promoted to {rank}", "Your commander is fully verified", and device security notices — "A device was paired to your account" and its removal twin, worth reading if it was not you.

"Squadron" is the shared feed of what members post, build and claim: new forum threads, new colonisation projects, "{title} is the squadron's current effort", completed projects, jackpot bounty claims, published ship builds, newly verified members, season champions and platinum ranks, and deploy announcements.

How reading works: clicking a personal row marks it read and follows its link (ctrl-click opens a tab and still marks it). "Mark all read" clears everything at once. The squadron feed has no per-row state — opening the tab is the read action, and the pill drops to zero.

Rows arrive live while you are on the site, newest first, thirty at a time, with "Show older" walking back as far as history goes.

Discord DMs are a separate, opt-in channel for the forum reasons (replies to you, mentions, followed threads) — each DM says why it came and where to turn it off.
