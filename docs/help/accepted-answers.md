title: Accepting an answer
surface: website
route: /forum
---
When a thread's question gets answered, the person who asked can say so — and that closes the loop for everyone who finds the thread later.

1. As the thread starter (or a moderator), find the reply that solved it.
2. Press the tick in its vote rail — "Mark as the answer".
3. The post's border turns cyan, and a copy of it floats to the top of the thread under the heading "ANSWERED", so nobody has to scroll to page three for the fix. The original stays in place.

A thread has one accepted answer at a time: accepting a different reply moves the mark. Pressing the tick on the accepted post un-accepts it.

Accepting pays XP on the first marking: 25 to the person whose answer it was, and 2 to you for closing your own question. Accepting your own answer awards nothing, and un-accepting never claws XP back. The answer's author is notified: "Your answer was accepted".

Accepted answers matter beyond the thread. They are the strongest correctness signal the forum can produce — somebody asked, somebody answered, and the person who asked confirmed it worked — so accepted answers are what the squadron's AI assistant learns from. (A post can also qualify by being heavily upvoted, at a net score of five or more, but acceptance qualifies outright.) Marking answers is quietly training the assistant to give the same fix to the next person who asks.
