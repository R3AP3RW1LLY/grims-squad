Trade Barons scores realized trading profit your journal reports: a point per ten thousand credits actually earned, sale matched against what you paid. Volume without margin scores nothing — that is the point.

"Realized" means the profit of an actual sale, priced against a real cost basis:

- The preferred basis is the game's own average price paid for that commodity, carried on the sale event — it covers every buy you ever made, including ones before the app existed.
- When the sale event lacks it, your own uploaded buys of that commodity in the 30 days before the sale supply an average.
- A sale with no honest basis — mined ore, mission cargo with no purchase trail — scores nothing rather than being guessed at.

Profit is sale minus cost, floored to whole points at 10,000 credits each. Loss-making sales are dropped, not subtracted: the board measures what you banked, and it never punishes an honest loss.

The honest limits: only sales your journal reports through the paired companion app can score, and scoring runs every few minutes against recent uploads — so the app needs to be running when you trade. Selling at any venue counts the same.

The page shows "Season {month}", "All-time" and "The tier ladder", with columns "#", "Runner", "Points" and "Sales", plus your own strip.

The Trade Barons ranks, by lifetime points on this board:

- "Courier" at 5,000
- "Merchant" at 50,000
- "Magnate" at 250,000
- "Trade Baron" at 1,000,000 — the board's own name, worn by whoever earns it

Achievements: "First Profit" (closed a first profitable trade the journal could vouch for), "Millionaire Run" (cleared a million credits of profit on a single sale), and "Trade Baron Champion" (topped the board for a whole season).
