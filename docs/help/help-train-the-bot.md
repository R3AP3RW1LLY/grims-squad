"Help Train the Bot", under GMSD AI in the sidebar, is the squadron's collection drive: screenshots that teach GMSD AI to draw Elite, and ship builds that teach it what a fitted ship looks like. Two tabs: "Generative AI (Images)" and "Ship build training".

On the images tab, "What the pool needs" shows nine category bars — ship exteriors, cockpits, stations, planetary surfaces, deep space, combat, SRV and on foot, scenery, miscellaneous — each filling towards the point where that category can be trained at all. To contribute:

1. In "Send a screenshot", pick the file: PNG, JPG or WebP, at least 1280px on the long edge. Undersized or undecodable files are refused before upload with the reason.
2. Pick the "Category" and write "What is in the shot" — 20 to 500 characters; the description is what teaches the model. "Ship in the shot" can stay blank — your companion app usually knows. Press "Send it".

Submissions land pending and only count towards a bar once a reviewer approves them; rejections carry the reviewer's note. "What you have sent" lists yours, with "Withdraw" on pending and approved items — withdrawing removes an image from future training, but cannot remove it from a model already trained; that is how the weights work, not a policy choice.

The ship-build tab collects loadouts by link. Paste a Coriolis or EDSY address into "Build link" and press "Read the build" — the entire loadout is decoded from the link itself; nothing is fetched from those sites. Short links (sh.orbis.zone, edsy.org/s/…) carry no build — open them and copy the long address instead. "What the pool still needs" tracks the role targets: Combat 60, Mining 40, Exploration 40, Trading 40, Multipurpose 30 — the role is read from the modules, never asked for.

Sending training material takes its own permission; the page still shows the collection's progress when your role lacks it.
