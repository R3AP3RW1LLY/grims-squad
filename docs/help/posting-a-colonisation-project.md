"Start New Project" posts a construction site so the squadron can help build it. Post it and your companion app keeps it up to date from then on — what it still needs, and who has hauled to it.

The easy path is the companion app's docked prefill:

1. Fly to your construction site and dock with the companion app running. The app reads the depot the game reports and fills the page in — name, system, station and market id, all of it, plus the site's current progress.
2. Open "Start New Project" in the app. The "Where you are" card shows the site and "{n}t still needed across {m} commodities".
3. Press "New project", check the fields, and post.

A dock is trusted for twelve hours; after that the page treats you as not docked. Docked somewhere that is not a construction site, it says so: "This is not a construction site. Dock at one and its needs appear here." If the site is already on the board, the form is replaced by a pointer to open it from "Squadron projects" or "Members' projects".

The hand-fill path works on the website and in the app when you are away from the game: fields "What to call it", "System", "Station (if it has a name yet)", "Market id" (the long number the game reports for the site — digits only) and "Anything the squadron should know", then "Post the project".

Officers see one more control: "Post as a squadron project — the whole squadron hauls for it". Without it, the project posts as your own, on the members' board. Posting at all needs the posting permission; the page says so plainly when it belongs to a higher rank.

A market id already on the board is refused — "That construction site is already posted as "{title}"." — so the same site is never posted twice.
