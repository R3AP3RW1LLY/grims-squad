---
title: Saving a build and choosing who sees it
surface: both
route: /shipyard
---
Under the slot tables sits "Save this build". Signed out, the space says instead: "Sign in with Discord to save this build and share it with the squadron."

1. Press "Save this build".
2. "Call it something" — a name up to 80 characters; blank falls back to the ship's own name.
3. Choose under "Who can see it":
   - "Only you" — "Kept against your account. Nobody else can open it."
   - "Anyone in the squadron" — "Signed-in members can open it, with your name on it. Set it back to private and it is gone."
   - "Anyone with the link" — "Works without signing in. Once somebody has the link you cannot take it back."
4. Press "Save". Shared builds get a "Share link" with a "Copy" button; the link is /shipyard/build/{token}.

Each level is its own permission on the rank ladder: saving at all, sharing with the squadron, and publishing outside the squadron are granted separately, and the refusals say which you are missing ("You cannot share builds with the squadron.", "You cannot publish builds outside the squadron."). "Only you" is pre-selected.

The link is the key: a public build's link works for anyone, signed in or not; a squadron build's link works only for signed-in members. Setting a build back to private makes its link stop working immediately — and sharing again restores the same link. Only the build's owner can change its visibility; an officer can remove a build, but publishing somebody's plan under their name is not moderation.

The first time a build is shared, the squadron feed announces it: "{Ship} build published" — "A fitted ship is now on the squadron shipyard for anybody to open and fly."

A shared build's page shows the figures recorded when it was shared, while the price is recomputed from today's catalogue — the page says exactly that. The companion app saves with the identical form and rules; it shows the link as a path with "Open the hub" beside it.
