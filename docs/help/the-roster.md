The roster is the squadron's own directory — every active member, what they fly, what they have mastered, and what time it is where they are. It is behind the sign-in and hidden from search engines.

Three tabs split the view: "All members", "Officers", "Members". The rail shows at-a-glance counts including "Verified CMDRs" and "Flew this week", plus "Your entry" with a "See how others see you" link to your public profile.

Each card shows:

- Your commander name (or display name until you verify), with "Commander" under a verified name, plus site roles.
- The "Squadron" block: member since, rank, awards, and the Inara badge — "Inara verified" means Inara confirms both the commander name and squadron membership; "Not verified" means it has not confirmed both halves yet.
- "Pilot ranks": all six ladders in a fixed grid, with Elite-grade ranks highlighted; the tooltip says whether they came from Inara or from the member's own game journal.
- "Flying" (current ship), "Timezone" with a live local clock, and either "Playing now" — shown while the companion app has heard from their game within the last five minutes — or "Last flew".

Privacy on the card is per field. "Last seen" (their last known system) appears only for members who turned on "Show my last known position"; ships, credits and activity are likewise governed by their own toggles in Commander Mgmt. Being listed at all is not a toggle: the roster lists every active member.

The page updates live — when somebody verifies or starts playing, the card changes without a reload.
