The "Build tracker" overlay keeps a colonisation build's needs on screen while you fly: the project title, the top four commodities still short (biggest shortfall first, in tonnes), and a progress bar captioned "{delivered} of {required} t · {pct}%" — plus "· {n} hauling" when crewmates are on it, and "· live from the squadron" when the numbers came from the hub.

Until it has something to track it reads "Mark a current build, or dock at a construction site."

To mark a current build:

1. Open the project — in the companion app or on the website — and join it from the "Crew" tab ("Join this build").
2. Tick the checkbox "This is my current build" on that tab.

You have one current build at a time: ticking it on another project moves the pin rather than adding a second. A finished build refuses the pin with "That build is already finished." Crewmates who have pinned the build show an "on this build" marker on the roster.

Where the numbers come from depends on where you are:

- Docked at your current build: the game's own depot reading, which updates every few seconds while you are docked — the freshest numbers there are.
- Anywhere else with a current build pinned: the whole project's needs and progress from the hub, every member's deliveries folded in, refreshed every 60 seconds (and immediately when you change the pin). This works even while sending is paused.
- No pin, but docked at a construction site: the depot reading alone, so the overlay is useful before a site is even posted.

Commodities that are finished drop off the list, and the top-four cap is what fits the panel — the project page carries the full list.
