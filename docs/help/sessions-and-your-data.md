The "Account" tab in Commander Mgmt shows where you are signed in, and everything the hub holds about you.

"Signed-in devices" lists every open session: the device, when it was last used and when it signed in (both in UTC), and a "This device" marker on the one you are reading from. Ending a session signs that device out immediately:

1. Find the session in the list. Unrecognised browsers show as "Unknown device".
2. Press "End session" — or "Sign out" on the row marked "This device", which signs you out here as well.
3. If you see something you do not recognise, end it and tell an officer.

Sessions also end on their own: each lasts 14 days from sign-in, and signing out from the account menu revokes the session server-side rather than just clearing the browser.

"Your data" holds the export. "Download my data" downloads everything the hub holds about you as a single JSON file: your profile, privacy settings, Discord link, roles, verified commander names, activity totals and sessions. Access tokens are excluded from the export.

The other half of your data — the journal telemetry the companion app has uploaded — is controlled from the "Companion app" page instead, where switching a category off also deletes what has already been stored under it.
