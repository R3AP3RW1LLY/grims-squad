Every project page opens with four tiles: "Still needed", "Delivered", "Commodities" and "Status". Status is one of three words: "Live", "Current effort", or "Complete".

"Current effort" is the squadron's priority build. Officers set it with the "Make current effort" button on a squadron project; when it switches on, the squadron is told — "Deliveries to {station}, {system} count double on the colonisation board." A personal project cannot be the current effort, and completion clears the flag automatically. On the boards, priority cards wear a "current effort" badge and sort first.

A freshly posted project may show no progress bar at all: "Waiting for somebody to dock there — the needs appear once a journal reports them." Needs come from the game's own depot report, refreshed every time somebody with the companion app docks at the site — the freshness line under the needs table says how long ago that was. Once needs are known, the page also identifies the build ("This is a {build type}"), linking to its build-types entry.

Projects close three ways on their own: the game reports construction complete, reports it failed, or every commodity line reaches delivered-equals-required. Whoever may direct the project — officers for a squadron build, the poster for a personal one — can also press "Close this build" (and "Reopen"). Closing ends the effort; the delivery ledger is untouched, because closing says nothing about cargo genuinely hauled. A finished build refuses new crew joins and refuses being marked as anyone's current build.

"Delete" appears only on a project the site has never reported a depot for — the mistyped-market-id case. A project with recorded deliveries refuses deletion: "People have already hauled to this build, and deleting it would erase their deliveries. Close it instead."
