Seasons are calendar months in UTC — the one clock every member's journal shares. Each board shows the running month by default, and the arrows beside "Season {month}" walk back through history; every season is its own URL, so standings can be pasted into Discord and everybody sees the same month. A month before the boards existed shows its empty table, which is the true answer. The companion app offers the last twelve months in a picker.

A season's champion is judged only after the month ends — a mid-month leader has won nothing yet. The top scorer of the completed month earns the board's champion badge ("Data Runner Champion", "Colony Builder Champion", "Trade Baron Champion"); an exact tie crowns everybody who tied. The result is announced to the squadron, and reaching a board's top rank gets its own announcement too.

Participation is yours to set, in Commander Mgmt's "What others can see" section:

1. "Include me on leaderboards" is the master switch — on for every commander; switching it off takes your name off every board at once.
2. The "Leaderboards" group beneath has one switch per board — "Data Runners", "Colony Builders", "Trade Barons" — each on for every commander, and each taking your name off that one board while it stays off.

Switching off removes your row entirely — the season table and the all-time roll both, everywhere the board appears, with everybody below you moving up a rank. It is removal, not anonymising: no blanked row discloses that somebody is hiding.

Your points are unaffected. They keep accruing while you are hidden, badges keep being earned, and your own strip on the board still shows your real score and where you would rank. Switch back on and your full history is simply there.
