"Ask GMSD AI", in the GMSD AI section of the sidebar, is the squadron's own assistant — answered from squadron data, with its sources shown. It searches what the squadron holds — systems and stations, live market prices, ships and modules, and our own guides — and answers from what it finds. When the answer is not in there it says so instead of guessing, which is the whole reason it is worth asking.

1. Type a question (up to 500 characters) and press "Ask". The example chips show the shape: "Where can I sell Painite?", "What does a Krait Mk II hold?", "What stations are within 30ly of Deciat?", "Where can I buy Tritium near Sol?"
2. The transcript shows "Searching squadron data…" and an answer typically lands in ten to fifteen seconds.
3. Under each answer, "{n} sources" expands to show exactly what was retrieved and where from — market data, galaxy data, ship catalogues, forum guides — with links where they exist.

It also works out what to fly: name a role and a budget or a hull ("what should I fly for mining with 50 million") and the answer comes from the same deterministic fitting engine as the AI assisted build, ship and cost first.

Follow-up questions work — the conversation's recent turns travel with each question, so "and where do I buy it" makes sense after an answer. There is a courtesy limit of 60 questions an hour.

The honest edges: when nothing relevant is found, the assistant says what it does know about rather than inventing an answer. When the model itself is unreachable, you get "The assistant is not reachable at the moment. The facts below are what I found." — with the retrieved sources still attached. And conversations are logged for officer review, which is how bad answers get found and fixed.

Accepted forum answers feed its knowledge, so marking solutions improves what it tells the next person.
