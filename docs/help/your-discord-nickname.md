Everyone in the squadron wears their verified Inara commander name in Discord, so the member list reads the same way in Discord as it does in game. The site sets and maintains the nickname for you.

Names are tidied to a house style:

- Each word is capitalised ("jean-luc picard" becomes "Jean-Luc Picard"; apostrophes count as word breaks, so "o'brien" becomes "O'Brien").
- Anything you write in "quotes" is treated as a callsign and rendered in capitals.
- Discord's 32-character limit applies; longer names are cut after tidying, so the preview matches what is set.

Officers, and members an officer has granted it to, can wear something different:

1. On the "YOUR NAME IN DISCORD" page (or the same chooser in Commander Mgmt), type into "Your nickname" — the placeholder shows what the convention would set.
2. Press "Save". The result reads "Saved, and your Discord nickname has been updated." — or explains why Discord could not be updated (the bot cannot rename the server owner, and Discord rate-limits renames).
3. Leave the field empty, or press "Use my Inara name", to go back to the convention name.

A chosen name stays until you change it — the automatic sweep leaves it alone, even if your Inara name changes later. Members without the override see their convention name stated on the page; if your name cannot be written the usual way, ask an officer, who can grant the override to anybody who needs it.
