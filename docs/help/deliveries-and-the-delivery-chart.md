Deliveries are recorded from the game itself. When you hand cargo over at a construction site, your companion app uploads the journal event and the project updates within seconds — before you have finished undocking. There is no hand-typed delivery entry: every recorded tonne was witnessed by a journal, which is why the ledger's empty state says deliveries "appear here within seconds of somebody with the companion app handing cargo over."

The project's "Deliveries" tab holds the ledger, "Every delivery": columns "Commodity", "Commander", "Tonnes" and "When", newest first, showing the 40 most recent on the website. Times are in your own timezone, and the caption under the table says exactly which: "Times in {zone}". (The companion app's ledger uses the clock of the machine it runs on — the device on your desk.)

Above it, "Deliveries over time" charts the effort three ways on one toggle:

- "by commodity" — time on the x-axis, bars stacked by what went in.
- "by commander" — the same bars, stacked by who put it in.
- "per commander" — one bar per person, stacked by what they brought.

Bars are one hour each, switching to one day when the project's span passes 48 hours, and only periods with deliveries get a bar. The caption states all of it: "One bar per hour · stacked by commodity · {n} hours with deliveries · times in {zone}". The buckets are cut in your stored timezone on the server — on both the website and the app — so an evening's deliveries never stack onto the wrong day's bar. Hovering a bar reports the whole stack with a total.

The "Haulers" tab, "Who has hauled", ranks the crew by tonnes delivered, with a footer totalling "{n} t delivered by {m} commanders."
