The website signs you in through Discord. There is no password on this site — your Discord account, and your membership of the Grim's Squad Discord server, are the whole of it.

1. On the public site, press "SQUADRON PORTAL SIGN IN" in the top bar. On a phone, open the slide-out menu and press it there.
2. Discord asks you to approve two scopes: your basic profile, and reading your membership of this one server. Your email address is never requested, and the site cannot read your messages or see your other servers.
3. Approve, and you land in the hub — your dashboard, or the next setup step if your account is new.

Signing in requires that you are already in the Grim's Squad Discord server. If you are not, the sign-in is refused with "You must be a member of the Grim's Squad Discord server to sign in." — use "JOIN GRIM'S SQUAD NOW" instead, which adds you to the server first.

Your name on the site comes from Discord, preferring your server nickname, then your global name, then your username.

A session lasts 14 days from the moment you sign in, and is not extended by activity; after 14 days you sign in again. If your Discord authorisation is removed, the session ends with it. Rank and permission changes take effect immediately — there is no need to sign out and back in.

To sign out, open the account menu (your name, top right) and press "Sign out". This ends the session everywhere, not just in that browser tab.
