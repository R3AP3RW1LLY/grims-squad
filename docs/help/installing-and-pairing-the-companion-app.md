The companion app runs on your gaming PC, reads your Elite Dangerous journals, and keeps your squadron profile, your ships and the squadron's market prices current.

To install and pair:

1. On the website, open "Companion app" in your personal sidebar section and download the build for your platform — Windows, macOS or Linux (the Mac and Linux builds read journals from CrossOver, Whisky or Proton).
2. Launch the app. Until it is paired, the window shows one thing: "Sign in with your squadron account to pair this machine. Your browser will open and ask you to approve it." Press "Sign in".
3. Your browser opens the "CONNECT THE APP" page, already filled in and showing this machine's name under "Asking to connect". Read the line that matters: connecting lets the app upload your Elite Dangerous journals to your account — it cannot read anything else, post as you, or change your settings.
4. Press "Connect this app". The page confirms "{device} is connected." and the app picks it up on its own within a couple of seconds — you can close the page.

If the browser page does not know the code (it asks "Enter the code your companion app is showing."), type the 8-character code the app is displaying into "Code from the app". A code lasts ten minutes; if it expires, press "Sign in" again for a fresh one.

Pairing switches sending on — signing in is the consent. To disconnect, "Unpair this device" in the app's Settings stops this machine, and the app will remind you: the squadron still trusts the token until you also press "Remove" next to the device on the website. Re-pairing is pressing "Sign in" again; reading resumes where it stopped, and anything re-sent is recognised as already held rather than duplicated.
