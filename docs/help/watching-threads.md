Following a thread means hearing about every new reply in it, not just replies to your own posts.

1. Open the thread and find the "Follow" panel in the right rail.
2. Press "Notify me". It changes to "Notifying you" while you follow.
3. Press it again to stop.

Nothing follows a thread for you automatically — posting or replying in a thread does not subscribe you. The button is the whole mechanism, which means your notifications only carry threads you chose.

What you receive: a row in the bell's "Personal" tab for each new reply. If you have linked Discord and turned on Discord DMs for this, the reply arrives as a DM instead — the panel's helper line tells you which is set: "You will get a Discord DM when somebody replies here." or "New replies will appear in your notifications." with a "Turn on Discord DMs" link.

One reply produces at most one notification, whichever ways you qualify. If somebody replies directly to your post, you are told as the reply target; if they mention you with "@", you are told as mentioned; following covers everything else. The person who wrote the reply is never notified about their own post.

A followed thread that gets moved to a board you cannot see stops notifying you — the check runs against your current access each time, so a follow can never leak a board you have lost.
