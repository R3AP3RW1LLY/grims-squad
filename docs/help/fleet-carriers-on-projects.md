---
title: Attaching fleet carriers to a build
surface: both
route: /colonisation/squadron
---
A fleet carrier attached to a colonisation project makes its hold count: whatever it carries of the build's outstanding commodities is subtracted from what still has to be bought and hauled. The "Carriers" tab is where this happens.

To attach one:

1. Use the search box — "callsign, or leave blank for whoever is carrying most". An empty search is the most useful one: press "Find carriers" and it lists the carriers holding the most of what this build still wants, ranked by matching tonnage.
2. Each match shows the carrier, its system, when its market was last seen, and "{n} t across {m} commodities".
3. Press "Add". Officers also see "Add as squadron", which marks it as the squadron's rather than a member's own.

Any member who can see the project can attach a carrier — a member offering their own carrier to somebody else's build is the case this exists for. Taking one off ("Take off") belongs to whoever added it, or an officer. A carrier nobody has ever reported a market for is refused: "Nobody has reported that carrier's market yet. Dock at it once and it will appear."

Once attached, the carrier's effective cargo shows up in three places, all from one calculation: the yellow "aboard attached carriers" segment on the needs bar with a "{n} t aboard" note, the tab's headline ("{n} t of the {m} t still wanted is already in a hold."), and the "Where to buy" tab's "To buy" and "Cost" figures.

Every carrier row shows its market's age ("seen today", "seen 3d ago" …); older than 30 days is flagged as a warning, because a carrier may have jumped away and its hold emptied since anyone looked.
