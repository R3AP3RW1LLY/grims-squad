The dashboard greets you by your own clock — "Good morning, commander" through "Good evening, commander" — and leads with the squadron's pulse: "{n} of {m} commanders have been seen this month."

Two activity sections mirror the bell's two tabs, so what needs doing is on the page you land on:

- "Your activity" — "What has just happened to you: badges, bounties, answers, your builds." The eight most recent rows from your personal feed. Reading them here marks nothing as read; the bell keeps its own count. Under it sits the rank-check footnote: Discord activity is counted automatically, and game sessions come from your paired devices.
- "Squadron activity" — "What has just happened across the squadron, and how the rest of us are doing." The ten most recent squadron events, each prefixed with who did it, above tiles for squadron size, "Active this month", "Verified commanders" and "Flying since".

The "Badges" section is your badge case — every badge you hold, forum and leaderboard both, each with its icon, name and when you earned it. Hover a badge for what it took. Until you have one, it names the three boards and notes the first tier lands at 500 lifetime points on any of them.

The rail carries "Your account" (commander, rank, and your companion app's version state), your session countdown, and — only while any apply — "Worth doing" items: "Verify your commander", "Secure your account", "Install the companion app".

The page refreshes itself as events arrive; nothing needs a reload.
