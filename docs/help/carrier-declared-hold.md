The "Declared hold" block on a project's "Carriers" tab is the honest answer to "what is actually aboard that carrier". It merges three sources:

- Journal figures — the owner's companion app watching cargo move on and off the carrier. A witness statement: it reports what it saw, starting fresh each time the app launches.
- Market mirror figures — the carrier's public sell orders, seen by the galaxy-wide market feed.
- Manual figures — typed by a crew member standing on the deck.

The merge rule is: manual beats journal beats mirror. The two automatic figures argue by size — the larger is the better floor — and a hand-typed figure beats both outright. A manual zero is a real statement, not an absence: it retires a stale automatic figure by saying the hold has none. The form's own hint reads "zero is a real figure — it says the hold has none".

To declare or correct a figure:

1. Join the build's crew — declaring a carrier's cargo is crew work, and rank does not substitute for it.
2. In "Declared hold", pick from "declare a commodity…" (it offers the build's outstanding commodities), type the tonnes, and press "Declare".
3. To correct an existing manual row, edit the number and press "Save". "Clear" removes the hand-typed figure so the journal and market readings stand again.

Rows are labelled by source: journal rows are read-only with an age chip, and when a manual row covers the same commodity they show "overridden" and are struck through — the crew member's figure below outranks them. Manual rows are tagged "by hand" with who set them and when.

Everything the declared hold resolves feeds the needs bar's "aboard attached carriers" segment and the shopping list's "To buy" subtraction — capped so a carrier holding more than the build wants covers the need, not more than it.
