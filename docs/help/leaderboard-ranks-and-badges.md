Every leaderboard has a four-rung ladder of themed ranks, earned on lifetime points for that board — whichever season they were earned in. The names are per-board, so a rank always says which board it was earned on:

- Data Runners: "Signal Scout" (500), "Wayfinder" (5,000), "Beacon Keeper" (25,000), "Void Cartographer" (100,000).
- Colony Builders: "Bricklayer" (1,000), "Foreman" (10,000), "Architect" (50,000), "Worldshaper" (200,000).
- Trade Barons: "Courier" (5,000), "Merchant" (50,000), "Magnate" (250,000), "Trade Baron" (1,000,000).

The thresholds differ because the points do: a tonne, a claim and ten thousand credits of profit are different sized deeds, and each ladder is tuned to its board.

Each rank is a badge, joined by achievement badges ("First Light", "Line Closer", "Millionaire Run", the season champion crowns, and the rest) and by the forum's own badges. Where they show:

- Your dashboard's badge case shows every badge you hold.
- Forum posts show up to three chips beside your name — rarest first, so your highest tier per board and any championships lead.
- Each leaderboard's "The tier ladder" section marks the rungs you have earned, and the companion app shows your tier chips with a progress bar to the next rank.
- Earning one rings the bell: "Badge earned: {name}".

Badges are never revoked — one earned under an old rule is history, not an error — and reaching a rank announces nothing you did not do: the award sweep reads the same catalogue the pages display, so the ladder can never say "earned" while the badge is missing.
