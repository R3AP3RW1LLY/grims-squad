title: Choosing what other members can see

The "What others can see" section on the "Commander settings" tab controls your profile field by field. Each item is separate — showing your position does not also show your balance — and changes save as you make them, so there is no button to forget. A change that fails to save reverts and tells you so.

The sharing switches, all off until you turn them on:

- "Show my last known position" — the system and station your commander was last seen at.
- "Show my credit balance" — most commanders leave this off.
- "Show my ships" — the ships you own and their names.
- "Show my activity" — message and voice totals. Officers see activity for rank progression regardless of this setting; the switch controls whether it is shown to everyone else.

When a switch is off, that field is absent from your profile entirely — not blanked, absent.

"Include me on leaderboards" is the master leaderboard switch. It is on for every commander; switching it off takes your name off every board at once. Below it, the "Leaderboards" group has one switch per board — "Data Runners", "Colony Builders", "Trade Barons" — each on for every commander, and each taking your name off that one board while it stays off. Opting out removes your row from the standings rather than anonymising it; your points still accrue and you still see your own score, so switching back on restores your full history.

One more switch, "Show all posts in the site font", is a reading preference for you only: commanders can pick from thirty fonts for their posts and signatures, and this renders everything in the plain site face on your screen. It changes nothing about how your own posts look to anybody else.

Appearing on the roster is not a switch — the roster is the squadron's own directory, behind the sign-in.
