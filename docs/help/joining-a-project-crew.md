---
title: Joining a project's crew
surface: both
route: /colonisation/squadron
---
Every colonisation project has a "Crew" tab — the roster of members hauling for it. Volunteering is not a privilege: any member who can see the project can join.

1. Open the project from "Squadron projects" or "Members' projects".
2. On the "Crew" tab, press "JOIN THIS BUILD". The same button place shows "LEAVE THIS BUILD" once you are on the roster — which button you see is the answer to whether you are on the build.

Being on the crew gets you three things:

- Your name on the roster, with your deliveries and your claimed commodities shown against it.
- The right to declare or correct an attached fleet carrier's hold — declaring a carrier's cargo is crew work, and the page will tell you to join first if you try without.
- The "This is my current build" checkbox, which appears once you have joined and drives the companion app's build tracker overlay.

The member who posted the project is notified when somebody joins ("{name} joined {title}"). Leaving is quieter: the poster is told the crew changed, deliberately without naming who left.

Leaving removes your claimed commodities from the roster; your recorded deliveries stay — they were genuinely hauled, and closing your involvement does not unhaul them. Joining a finished build is refused with "That build is already finished."

Crewmates who have marked the project as their current build carry an "on this build" tag on the roster, so you can see who is actively flying it right now rather than just signed up.
