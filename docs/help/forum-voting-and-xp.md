title: Voting, XP and forum badges

Every post carries a vote rail on its left: an up arrow, the score ("+3"), and a down arrow. Every member can vote — there is no rank gate. You cannot vote on your own post; pressing the same arrow again withdraws your vote, and pressing the other one changes it. The small "👍 Upvote" button in a post's footer is a different thing: a visible reaction on the post, not a vote.

Votes feed XP, and the numbers say what the squadron values — not volume, but posts other people found worth something:

- Writing a post: 2 XP. Starting a thread: 3 XP.
- Somebody upvoting your post: 10 XP to you.
- Somebody downvoting your post: −2 XP. Deliberately small — being wrong in public should not be expensive — and the voter pays nothing to say it.
- Your answer accepted as the solution: 25 XP, the largest single award. Accepting an answer on your own thread: 2 XP.
- A day the telemetry saw you in the game: 5 XP — per day, not per event.

XP places you on the level ladder shown with your name: "Recruit" (0), "Cadet" (50), "Pilot" (200), "Wingman" (500), "Veteran" (1,000), "Elite" (2,500), "Legend" (5,000). XP is not clamped at zero — a consistently downvoted record shows honestly.

The forum's badges are earned for things that helped somebody else: "First Answer" (one accepted answer), "Navigator" (ten), "Wing Commander" (fifty), "Well Received" (25 upvotes across your posts), "Squadron Voice" (200), "Regular" (30 days flying on record), "Veteran" (365 days). There is no badge for posting a hundred times, because a badge for volume is an instruction to post volume.

Beside your name on posts, up to three badge chips show — your rarest first, drawn from both the forum badges and the leaderboard ranks.
