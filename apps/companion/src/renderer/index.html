What gets sent

Always: when you start the game, your ranks and progress, your squadron standing, and your ships. That is what the squadron runs on, and it is what the monthly rank check reads.

Only if you ask: where you jumped, what you fought, what you traded, what you explored. Off unless you turn it on for the leaderboards, on the website.

Never: your chat, your friends list, how you died, or your credit balance. The filtering happens on this PC, before anything leaves it — and the button below shows you exactly what a batch looks like from your own files.
